# Le Moulin de Laure — Brand & Website Design Brief

Status: **client brief received; visual foundations approved**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

This is the normalized design/product brief. The approved visual implementation lives in `MASTER.md`.

## Business

Brand: **Le Moulin de Laure**

Website: https://lemoulindelaure.fr

Primary activity / universes:
1. Communication animalière
2. Accompagnement énergétique animalier
3. Connexion avec les défunts
4. Guidance pour soi

Related editorial/network space:
- Le Jardin du Moulin

## Business goals

- explain what Laure offers and why;
- make the activity visible;
- build trust and understanding;
- generate qualified appointments;
- initial stated goal: 8–10 appointments/month.

Target launch from source brief: beginning of January 2027.

## Audience

Primary:
- adult animal guardians;
- people interested in communication animalière.

Additional:
- people seeking connection/messages related to deceased humans;
- breeders/caretakers and other professionals;
- people drawn to guidance/connection work.

## Top user intents

1. Understand what each accompaniment is.
2. Decide which accompaniment matches the current need.
3. Reassure themselves about Laure's approach, values and boundaries.
4. Book an appointment.
5. Ask a question/contact Laure.

## Conversion

Primary conversion:
- appointment booking through Timetics via `/prendre-rendez-vous/`.

Secondary conversions:
- contact form;
- reading FAQ / reassurance content;
- Journal discovery;
- exploring Laure's story/expertise.

## Constraints

- no WordPress core modification;
- no Astra parent-theme modification;
- no React/Tailwind/shadcn layer unless explicitly approved;
- preserve SEO/schema/privacy/form/booking behavior;
- prioritize performance, accessibility and maintainability;
- no medical/veterinary diagnosis or treatment claims;
- preserve client artwork/copyright treatment.
