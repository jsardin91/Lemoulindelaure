# Le Moulin de Laure — Working Brand Guidelines

Status: **authoritative client brief + approved visual foundations**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

This file normalizes the written client brief and project decisions into a durable reference.

It does not replace future explicit client corrections. Later approved decisions supersede this file where they conflict.

## Brand essence

Brand: **Le Moulin de Laure**

Signature/motto:

**Au cœur du lien, au-delà des sens.**

Core ideas expressed by the client:
- link;
- living beings;
- communication;
- heart;
- spirit;
- soul;
- source;
- connection beyond ordinary senses.

The experience should remain grounded and understandable even when the subject matter is spiritual or intuitive.

## Website purpose

The website should:
- help visitors discover and understand what Laure offers;
- explain why she follows this path and what motivates her;
- make the activity visible;
- inspire confidence and understanding;
- generate appointments.

Initial objective stated by the client: **8 to 10 appointments per month**.

Appointment availability: mainly evenings and Saturdays.

## Four universes

### 1. Écureuil — Terre — Vivant
**Communication animalière**

Belief/intention: better understanding an animal can deepen understanding of the bond with it.

### 2. Phénix — Feu — Énergie
**Accompagnement énergétique animalier**

Belief/intention: caring for living beings may also involve an energetic approach.

### 3. Tortue — Eau — Famille
**Connexion avec les défunts**

Belief/intention: some bonds may continue when physical presence has disappeared.

### 4. Papillon — Air — Messager
**Guidance pour soi**

Belief/intention: some things may be felt before they can be explained and may open new ways of understanding a situation or life path.

### Connecting layer
Heart / ether / love: the link surrounding the four universes.

## Le Jardin du Moulin

A separate editorial/network space.

Purpose:
- present selected experts/therapists/professionals encountered by Laure;
- show that different viewpoints can complement rather than oppose each other;
- introduce each person/activity briefly and provide contact details with consent.

Potential groups:
- humans;
- animals.

This is not a fifth core accompaniment.

## Audience

- adult animal guardians (18+) with dogs, cats, birds, horses or other animals;
- people seeking animal communication;
- people wishing to receive a message connected to deceased humans;
- professionals such as breeders/caretakers;
- people who feel called by the wider approach.

## Values

- Bienveillance
- Empathie
- Non-jugement
- Amour du vivant
- Humilité
- Honnêteté
- Respect
- Confidentialité de l’échange

## Differentiation / posture

The animal is considered a being in its own right, with sensitivity, history and needs.

Respect applies equally to the animal and the person accompanying it.

Each situation is welcomed without judgment.

What is perceived should be communicated with sincerity and softness.

Communication animalière should not be framed simply as “getting answers”; it is presented as a bridge between human and animal that can offer another perspective on their relationship.

## Medical / veterinary boundary

Mandatory principle:

Laure is neither a veterinarian nor a doctor and does not make medical diagnoses.

When medical/veterinary attention is necessary, competent professionals remain essential.

No site copy, UI or SEO content should imply diagnosis, treatment, guaranteed healing or medical efficacy.

## Expertise

Client source states:
- training in communication animalière;
- diploma/training at the school of Laila Del Monte;
- four years of training.

Before publishing detailed credential wording, verify the exact official diploma/title spelling supplied by the client.

## Modalities

Client brief mentions:
- appointments at a distance or in person;
- individual or group format for messages where relevant;
- communication animalière can take place over approximately three days.

Exact commercial terms, durations and pricing must be confirmed per offer before publication.

## Voice and writing

- French
- vouvoiement
- accessible to a broad audience
- primarily informative
- warm and human
- gentle and sincere
- small touches of humour are welcome
- avoid jargon-heavy or overly mystical phrasing
- stay grounded (“terre à terre”) to avoid alienating visitors

## Visual personality

Desired impression:
- intuitive;
- joyful;
- kind;
- soft;
- colorful;
- energetic;
- professional;
- easy to read;
- not strict or overly classical;
- grounded;
- animal at the forefront.

The site may cover sensitive/emotional subjects, so energy/color should coexist with calm and emotional respect.

Avoid the visual “common trunk” of generic AI websites.

Personalized icons are preferred.

- preserve adequate visual breathing room around the mark.

## Door concept

The project uses doors opening toward different universes as a reusable brand/navigation device.

Rules:
- doors can introduce or accompany service navigation;
- doors must not replace clear textual service names;
- interactions must remain accessible and keyboard-friendly;
- avoid mandatory reveal/scroll gimmicks;
- motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.
