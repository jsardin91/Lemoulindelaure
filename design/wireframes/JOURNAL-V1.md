# Journal — Index + Article Template V1

Status: **working design draft — editorial architecture only**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Public label:
**Journal**

Canonical URLs:
- index: `/journal/`
- article: `/journal/[slug]/`

## Purpose

The Journal should:
- build expertise and reassurance;
- support SEO and discoverability;
- answer real visitor questions;
- create useful internal links toward accompaniment pages;
- deepen the voice and worldview of Le Moulin.

It should not exist merely to publish on a schedule.

## Launch rule

Do not expose an empty Journal index.

Do not create public categories/tags until the editorial/SEO strategy justifies them.

---

# Journal index

## 1. Hero

Compact editorial hero.

Content:
- H1: **Journal**
- one sentence explaining what readers will find there;
- no oversized CTA;
- no category filter at launch unless real category depth exists.

Visual:
- subtle original artwork/detail;
- mostly typography.

## 2. Featured article

The index gets the more expressive layout.

Desktop:
- featured article ~7/12;
- large image;
- title;
- short excerpt;
- published date;
- optional reading time if implemented reliably.

The featured item should be editorially chosen, not automatically “latest” unless that is intentionally the rule.

## 3. Supporting article list

Prefer:
- 2-column editorial list;
- or stacked split-image rows.

Avoid:
- 3 identical cards if content length varies;
- masonry for text-heavy articles;
- tag/filter UI before enough content exists.

Each entry:
- image;
- title;
- date;
- concise excerpt;
- clear link.

## 4. Pagination

When needed:
- standard pagination;
- accessible previous/next;
- no infinite scroll by default.

# Article template

## Header

Content order:
1. optional category only if approved strategy exists;
2. H1;
3. short standfirst/excerpt;
4. published date;
5. updated date when materially updated;
6. hero image if relevant.

Do not clutter with share controls above the article.

## Reading column

Target line length:
**60–75 characters** desktop.

Body:
- Source Sans 3;
- ~18px desktop where comfortable;
- line-height ~1.6–1.75;
- generous heading spacing.

Heading structure:
- one H1;
- H2/H3 sequentially;
- never skip levels to achieve visual size.

## Content components

Allowed:
- paragraphs;
- lists;
- pull quote;
- original artwork/image;
- FAQ subsection when genuinely useful;
- callout;
- related accompaniment link.

Avoid:
- excessive colored boxes;
- repeated CTA between paragraphs;
- fake expert quotes;
- decorative icon lists everywhere.

## Images

Every content image:
- meaningful alt text where appropriate;
- explicit dimensions/aspect ratio;
- optimized responsive formats;
- caption/source if needed.

Original Laure artwork:
- preserve copyright treatment;
- do not expose full-resolution source unnecessarily.

## Internal linking

Every article should have a natural content path:
- related accompaniment;
- FAQ;
- About;
- another relevant Journal article.

Do not force a booking CTA if the article topic does not support it.

## End-of-article area

Recommended:
1. related accompaniment/contextual next step;
2. 2–3 related articles;
3. optional booking/contact CTA only where relevant.

Avoid giant generic CTA on every article.

# SEO / editorial rules

Before publishing an article:
- one primary search/user intent;
- unique title;
- unique meta description;
- correct H1;
- structured headings;
- useful answer in first paragraphs;
- clear author attribution where appropriate;
- honest published date;
- updated date when materially revised;
- canonical URL;
- no thin posts made just to target keywords.

No AI-generated article should be published without human/client editorial review.

## Medical/sensitive topics

For animal health, energetic or grief-related content:
- do not make medical/veterinary claims;
- do not convert beliefs into universal facts;
- clearly frame Laure’s own perspective where relevant;
- avoid exploitative or fear-based titles.

# Visual direction

Index:
more magazine-like and image-led.

Article:
quiet, highly readable and restrained.

This intentionally follows the principle:
**the index gets the design; the article gets read.**

Palette:
- Cream/Paper dominant;
- Ink typography;
- article-specific artwork accents;
- no separate blog theme.

---

# Mobile

Index:
- featured first;
- supporting articles stacked;
- no horizontal card carousels.

Article:
- full-width reading column with safe gutters;
- body >=16px;
- no oversized H1 that creates 5–6 lines;
- media never creates horizontal scroll.

---

# 21st adaptation

Useful:
- responsive blog index;
- split image blog list;
- editorial grid;
- plain article reading system.

Rejected:
- glass blog cards;
- infinite marquee;
- hover-only previews on mobile;
- overbuilt filter UI;
- social-share button walls.
