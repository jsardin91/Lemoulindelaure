# Communication animalière — Page Wireframe V1

Status: **working design/content-architecture draft — source-grounded, not final copy**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

URL:
`/accompagnements/communication-animaliere/`

Universe:
**Écureuil — Terre — Vivant**

Client intention:
**Mieux comprendre son animal, c'est mieux comprendre le lien qui nous unit.**

## Source-supported facts currently available

The client brief supports:
- audience includes adult guardians of animals such as dogs, cats, birds and horses
- professionals such as breeders/caretakers can also be part of the audience
- communication animalière is described as another way of communicating/understanding the animal relationship
- Laure considers the animal a being in its own right with sensitivity, history and needs
- Laure reports four years of training at the school of Laila Del Monte
- work can be remote or in person
- communication animalière can take time over approximately three days
- Laure is not a veterinarian or doctor and does not diagnose
- competent professionals remain essential when needed
- initial FAQ includes why communication animale, session flow, remote/in-person, and whether the animal feels something

Not yet supported with exact public details:
- price
- exact step-by-step operational process
- exact deliverables
- exact appointment/session duration
- exact preparation/material required
- cancellation policy
- exact official diploma title/spelling

These must not be invented.
