# Guidance pour soi — Page Wireframe V1

Status: **working design/content-architecture draft — source detail limited**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

URL:
`/accompagnements/guidance-pour-soi/`

Universe:
**Papillon — Air — Messager**

Client intention:
**Certaines choses se ressentent avant de pouvoir s’expliquer, et elles peuvent ouvrir de nouvelles voies de compréhension à une situation ou à un parcours de vie.**

## Source-supported facts currently available

The client brief supports:
- this is one of the four core accompaniment universes;
- identity: papillon / air / messager;
- the purpose is framed around felt/perceived things that may open new ways of understanding a situation or life path;
- preliminary vocabulary includes connexion / channeling;
- site-level appointments can be remote or in person;
- appointments are mainly evenings/Saturdays;
- values include benevolence, empathy, non-judgment, humility, honesty, respect and confidentiality;
- tone must remain informative, accessible and grounded;
- the client explicitly wants to avoid becoming “too spiritual” visually/verbally.

Not currently supported:
- exact process;
- exact questions/situations accepted;
- whether this is predictive, intuitive, symbolic or channelled in a specific way;
- duration;
- price;
- preparation;
- frequency;
- guarantees;
- decision-making claims;
- dedicated FAQ answers.

Do not fill these gaps from tarot/psychic/coaching/general spiritual content.

# 1. Hero — Air / Messager

## Page job

Present Guidance pour soi as a space for another perspective or understanding, not as a prediction service.

Desktop:
- asymmetric split;
- text ~6 columns;
- butterfly artwork ~5 columns;
- generous negative space.

Identity:
**Papillon · Air · Messager**

H1:
**Guidance pour soi**

Support:
plain-language sentence based directly on the client's idea of opening new ways of understanding a situation or life path.

CTA:
**Prendre rendez-vous**

Secondary anchor:
**Découvrir l’approche** or equivalent, to validate later.

## Visual direction

Lead:
- original butterfly painting.

Palette emphasis:
- Lavender;
- Paper;
- Ink;
- small Moulin blue/Teal support.

Visual character:
- lighter and airier than the other three pages;
- more negative space;
- gentle vertical movement in composition;
- no mystical cosmic imagery.

Avoid:
- crystal balls;
- tarot cards unless Laure later explicitly uses them;
- constellations;
- zodiac;
- third-eye graphics;
- fortune-telling cues;
- neon aura/glow.

Motion:
- subtle 4–8px butterfly/art crop drift or opacity reveal;
- no continuous flutter animation.

Reduced motion:
static.

Mobile:
message first, image second.

# 2. “Un autre éclairage”

## Purpose

Explain the service in source-safe language.

Core idea:
some things may be felt before they can be explained, and those perceptions may open other ways of understanding a situation or life path.

Framing rule:
keep statements attributed to Laure’s approach rather than presenting intuition/channeling as objective fact.

Layout:
editorial statement + short prose.

Do not use:
- “obtenir les réponses”;
- “connaître votre avenir”;
- “savoir quoi faire”;
- “recevoir la vérité”;
- certainty/prediction language.

Therefore:

No card grid.

---

# 4. “Ce que cette guidance n’est pas”

This is a **project content/UX clarification**, not direct client-source wording.

Useful because “guidance” can be interpreted as predictive or directive.

Recommended boundaries for owner/client validation:
- not a guaranteed prediction;
- not a decision made in the visitor’s place;
- not a certainty about future events;
- not medical/legal/financial advice.

These points should not be published until the client agrees with the exact framing, but the design should reserve a calm clarification area.

Visual:
small Paper/Ink band with simple prose, not warning UI.

# Design distinction from other detail pages

Communication animalière:
grounded / earth / relationship.

Énergétique:
warm / fire / energy.

Défunts:
quiet / water / memory / family.

Guidance:
**air / openness / perspective / movement**.

This should be expressed through:
- more white/cream space;
- lighter image placement;
- restrained Lavender;
- less dense section backgrounds;
- typography and composition, not cliché symbols.

---

# 21st / component strategy

21st guidance followed:
use one memorable visual moment and keep the rest readable/plain.

Useful families:
- Split Hero With Image Cards — source-order/layout logic only;
- Editorial Collage / image stacks — very restrained;
- plain content sections;
- minimal FAQ;
- minimal CTA.

Rejected:
- oracle/tarot-inspired generic component styling;
- floating glass cards;
- aurora gradients;
- kinetic mystical typography;
- WebGL/particle backgrounds.
