# À propos — Page Wireframe V1

Status: **working design/content-architecture draft — source-grounded**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

URL:
`/a-propos/`

## Page job

Build trust by showing:
- who Laure is;
- why this work matters to her;
- how her path developed;
- what values frame her practice;
- what training supports the communication animalière offer.

This page should feel human and personal, not like a résumé or “personal brand” landing page.

## Source-supported story

The client brief states:
- Laure has long believed there may be another way to communicate with animals beyond spoken language, looks and gestures;
- she wanted a way to better understand situations, accompany animals, become closer and facilitate daily relationships;
- after encouraging results, she developed her abilities first for her own animals, then for relatives and friends;
- today her activity extends beyond that circle;
- she has accompanied several animals and their guardians, notably through communication animalière;
- she reports four years of training at the school of Laila Del Monte;
- her values include benevolence, empathy, non-judgment, love of living beings, humility, honesty, respect and confidentiality;
- she considers the animal a being in its own right, with sensitivity, history and needs;
- she is neither veterinarian nor doctor and does not diagnose.

## Missing / to verify before publication

- exact years/dates of the journey;
- exact diploma title;
- exact official spelling/name of the training organisation;
- any other training/certifications;
- professional portrait;
- precise number of clients/animals accompanied if Laure wants to state it.

Do not invent dates or a chronological timeline.

# 1. Hero — Laure first

- negative space: 1/12.

Source order:
1. H1 / introduction;
2. short statement;
3. portrait.

H1:
**À propos de Laure** or final SEO/content wording.

Support:
a concise human introduction, not a list of credentials.

Primary action:
**Découvrir les accompagnements**

Booking can remain in header/global navigation; it does not need to dominate this page hero.

## Visual

Use real professional portrait once supplied.

Until then:
- internal placeholder only;
- do not generate a fake portrait.

Background:
Cream/Paper with Ink/Blue type.

# 2. “Pourquoi ce chemin ?”

## Purpose

Tell the origin story in prose.

Layout:
- large editorial heading;
- 2–4 short paragraphs;
- optional original animal painting detail.

Story structure:
1. long-standing intuition/belief about another way to communicate with animals;
2. desire to better understand and accompany;
3. first experiences with her own animals;
4. extension to relatives/friends;
5. work now reaching beyond that circle.

No fake dates.

No numbered timeline.

---

# 3. “De l’intuition à la formation”

## Purpose

Connect personal story to structured learning.

Supported:
- four years of training;
- school of Laila Del Monte;
- diploma/training wording to verify.

Design:
- typographic “4 ans de formation” fact;
- short supporting explanation;
- optional diploma image later if supplied/approved.

Do not:
- use certification badges;
- use logo of an institution unless usage is permitted;
- overstate credential scope.

21st note:
a history timeline is rejected because the source does not contain a complete dated sequence.

---

# 4. “Ma façon d’accompagner”

## Purpose

Turn values into practice, not decorative badges.

Supported posture:
- animal respected as a being in its own right;
- equal respect for animal and guardian;
- no judgment;
- sincerity;
- softness;
- confidentiality.

Layout:
editorial two-column:
- left: one large statement;
- right: concise principles.

Avoid:
- eight icon cards;
- generic “values” grid.

---

# 5. “Au cœur du lien”

## Purpose

Connect Laure's personal philosophy back to the brand.

Content:
- communication animalière as more than “getting answers”;
- a bridge between animal and human;
- desire for stronger bond, more complicity and a lighter heart.

Design:
full-width brand moment with signature:
**Au cœur du lien, au-delà des sens.**

This is allowed to be visually memorable.

Use one original artwork or emblem.

---

# Responsive

Mobile:
- text intro first;
- portrait second;
- story remains prose, not alternating image rows;
- training fact stays prominent;
- values stack naturally.

Avoid overlong decorative sections.

---

# Accessibility

- portrait alt only if it conveys information not already in adjacent text; otherwise empty alt can avoid duplication;
- real heading hierarchy;
- long prose max 65–75ch;
- no timeline semantics without real chronology;
- no decorative quote marks read by screen readers.
