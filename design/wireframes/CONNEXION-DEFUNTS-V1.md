## Source-supported facts currently available

The client brief supports:
- this is one of the four core accompaniment universes;
- identity: tortue / eau / famille;
- the public audience includes adults wishing to receive a message from deceased humans;
- preliminary vocabulary includes connexion / channeling / messages de l’au-delà / êtres de lumière;
- appointments may be remote or in person at site level;
- “messages” may be individual or group format;

- “messages” are described as easier to schedule than communication animalière;
- values include benevolence, empathy, non-judgment, honesty, respect and confidentiality;
- site tone should remain informative, accessible and grounded rather than excessively spiritual;
- the client explicitly notes that some subjects can be sensitive, emotional and trying for visitors.

Not currently supported:

- claims about certainty, proof, signs or outcomes.

Do not fill these gaps from general spiritual/mediumship content.

# 1. Hero — Eau / Famille

## Page job

Introduce the accompaniment with dignity and emotional clarity.

The first screen should communicate:
- the theme is the continuing bond after physical absence;
- Laure’s approach is gentle and confidential;
- no certainty or guaranteed message is promised.

## Desktop composition

Asymmetric split:
- copy ~6 columns;
- turtle painting ~5 columns;
- one column breathing room.

Identity line:
**Tortue · Eau · Famille**

H1:
**Connexion avec les défunts**

Support:
short, sensitive wording based on the client belief that some bonds continue even when physical presence is gone.

CTA:
- primary: **Prendre rendez-vous**
- secondary: optional anchor **Comprendre cet accompagnement**

## Visual direction

Lead asset:
- original turtle painting.

Palette emphasis:
- Moulin blue;
- Teal;
- Paper/Cream;
- small Lavender accents if useful.

Avoid:
- black backgrounds;
- candles;
- grave imagery;
- stars/galaxies as default;
- mist/smoke;
- “portal to the afterlife” clichés;
- glowing silhouettes;
- angel wings;
- séance imagery.

This page should feel like **water, memory, family and connection**, not death imagery.

## Motion

Very restrained:
- subtle water-like crop movement or opacity shift is acceptable;
- no floating spirits, particles or paranormal effects.

Reduced motion:
static final composition.

## Mobile

Source order:
1. identity;
2. H1;
3. support;
4. CTA;
5. turtle artwork.

# 7. Sensitive-content editorial guardrail

This is a **project UX/content guardrail**, not a statement supplied by the client.

Do not position the service as:
- proof of an afterlife;
- guaranteed contact;
- a guaranteed path to closure;
- a replacement for professional grief or mental-health support.

Do not use fear, urgency, guilt or vulnerability to drive booking.

Do not write:
- “they are trying to contact you”;
- “don’t miss their message”;
- “book now before the connection closes”;
- similar pressure tactics.

The site may acknowledge emotion without exploiting it.
