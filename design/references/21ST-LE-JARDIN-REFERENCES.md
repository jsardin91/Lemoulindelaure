# 21st Reference Study — Le Jardin du Moulin

Status: **reference only**

Last updated: 2026-10-02

## Goal

Use people/profile patterns as inspiration for a curated network while avoiding the feel of a corporate team page or commercial marketplace.

## Useful 21st guidance

### Team Sections — trust signal

Reference:
https://docs.21st.dev/blog/react-team-section-components

Useful principles:
- use real people;
- real photos rather than stock;
- meaningful descriptions/titles;
- link out where possible;
- visual treatment should support verification/trust, not decoration.

This maps well to Le Jardin, even though the people are not a “team”.

### Magazine-style Team Showcase

Referenced in the 21st Team Sections guide.

Useful:
- staggered photo layout;
- editorial rhythm;
- less corporate than an equal grid.

Adaptation:
- small curated set;
- approved profiles only;
- humans/animals categorization;
- no employee language.

### Plain profile/grid patterns

Useful when participant count grows.

Adaptation:
- keep profile data concise;
- real outbound links;
- no rating/pricing marketplace affordances.

## Explicitly rejected

- Team carousel;
- kinetic animated roster;
- hover-only contact information;
- stock portraits;
- fake “verified” badges;
- rating stars;
- marketplace filters before the dataset justifies them;
- map view without a real location use case;
- “Book this practitioner” through Laure’s Timetics.

## Future MCP search prompts

- editorial team showcase
- magazine profile grid
- simple profile list
- staggered people grid
- accessible team section

Preview candidates first and strip corporate/team semantics before adaptation.
