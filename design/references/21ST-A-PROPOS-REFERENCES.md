## Useful current guidance

### Timeline guide

Reference:
https://news.21st.dev/blog/react-timeline-components

Important principle:
a timeline is useful when the content is genuinely sequential and supported by real events/dates.

Decision:
**Do not use a timeline yet.**

The current client brief describes a journey but does not provide a dated, complete chronology.

### Team / person trust guidance

Reference:
https://docs.21st.dev/blog/react-team-section-components

Useful principle:
real people, real photos, meaningful information and checkable details build trust.

Adaptation:
- real Laure portrait only;
- exact training wording;
- no stock/fake portrait;
- no invented role/achievement data.

### Portfolio / personal-page guidance

Reference:
https://docs.21st.dev/blog/react-portfolio-site-components

Useful principle:
a concise statement of who someone is and what they do beats decorative self-branding.

Adaptation:
- clear introduction;
- actual story;
- real constraints/facts;
- no generic “passionate about…” filler.
