## Explicitly rejected

- star fields / cosmic backgrounds;
- shader portals;
- dark mystical hero sections;
- ghost/angel illustrations;
- testimonial marquees;
- animated particle fields;
- countdown/urgency CTA patterns;
- grief “success stories” as conversion devices.

## 21st research principle

The page should borrow **editorial quietness**, not “spiritual website” visual clichés.

Future MCP search terms:
- minimal editorial statement
- calm split content
- sensitive storytelling layout
- minimal faq
- quiet image text section
