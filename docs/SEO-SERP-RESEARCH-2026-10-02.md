# SEO SERP Research — Le Moulin de Laure

Date: **2026-10-02**

Status: **qualitative research, not keyword-volume data**

## Method

This research compares the current French-language SERP wording and page intent for the core topics of Le Moulin de Laure.

Important limitation:
- no Google Ads Keyword Planner / Ahrefs / Semrush volume data was available in this research pass;
- conclusions below are based on current SERP language, result types and intent patterns;
- therefore they guide wording/architecture, not traffic forecasts.

## Source hierarchy

1. Client brief and approved project terminology
2. Current official source material where a credential/provider is named
3. Current Google Search documentation
4. Current competitor/SERP observations

External SEO wording must never overwrite a client fact or introduce a service claim that Laure has not approved.

---

# 1. Communication animale

## SERP observation

The dominant public phrase is clearly:

**communication animale**

Common secondary formulations:
- communication animale à distance
- praticienne / communicatrice animale
- communication animale chien / chat / cheval
- comment se déroule une communication animale
- qu'est-ce que la communication animale

“Communication animalière” exists as natural French wording but is less dominant in current search-result titles/H1s.

Representative current results:
- Laila Del Monte
- Perceptions Animales
- Cats4life
- Communication Animale Laurence
- practitioner/directory pages

## SEO implication

Primary search wording should be:
**communication animale**

Recommended secondary cluster:
- communication animale à distance
- communicatrice animale / praticienne en communication animale
- communication animale chien
- communication animale chat
- communication animale cheval
- séance de communication animale
- comprendre son animal

Only use specific situations/use-cases if Laure supplies or approves them.

## Architecture recommendation

Current project label:
“Communication animalière”

SEO recommendation before implementation:
- public H1: **Communication animale**
- recommended slug: `/accompagnements/communication-animale/`
- nav label can also become “Communication animale”

This change is not yet treated as canonical until owner/client wording approval.

Reason:
- aligns with the client's own preliminary keyword “Communication animale”;
- better matches current SERP language;
- avoids an unnecessary linguistic mismatch before URLs are created.

# 2. Accompagnement énergétique animalier

## SERP observation

Current results frequently use:
- soins énergétiques animaux
- soin énergétique pour animaux
- soin énergétique animalier
- soin énergétique à distance animaux

“Magnétisme animalier” is also present in search, but that is a different/practitioner-specific modality and must **not** be imported into Laure's page unless she explicitly practices and names it.

## SEO implication

Keep the brand-safe service wording:
**Accompagnement énergétique animalier**

Potential semantic/search phrases:
- accompagnement énergétique pour animaux
- soin énergétique pour animaux
- soins énergétiques animaux
- soin énergétique animalier

The client's brief itself lists “soins énergétiques pour les animaux”, so that phrase may be used in body copy if Laure approves final wording.

Do not target symptom/illness queries or promise physical/emotional effects unless explicitly supported and safely worded.

# 3. Connexion avec les défunts

## SERP observation

Current result language commonly includes:
- communication avec les défunts
- contact défunt
- contact avec un défunt
- messages d'un défunt
- médium / médiumnité

The term **médium** is common in the SERP, but Laure's current brief does not explicitly define her public professional title as “médium”.

## SEO implication

Keep:
**Connexion avec les défunts**

Use semantically where source-safe:
- communication avec les défunts
- message d'un défunt / messages de défunts
- lien avec un défunt

Do **not** optimize Laure as a “médium” unless she explicitly approves that professional label.

Do not create “deuil therapy/support” search pages unless Laure actually provides that service.

# 4. Guidance pour soi

## SERP observation

The current dominant phrase is:
**guidance intuitive**

Common page intent:
- éclairer une situation
- prendre du recul
- mieux comprendre ce qui se joue
- retrouver de la clarté

Many results explicitly distinguish guidance from prediction/voyance, which aligns well with our current UX guardrail.

“Guidance pour soi” is much less visible as an established search phrase.

## SEO implication

Keep client service name:
**Guidance pour soi**

Recommended secondary phrase **pending Laure approval**:
- guidance intuitive

Potential public phrasing after approval:
“Guidance pour soi — un éclairage intuitif sur une situation ou un parcours”

Do not introduce:
- tarot
- oracle
- voyance
- prediction
- future-reading
unless Laure actually uses and approves those methods/terms.

## Google FAQ rich-result status

As of 2026:
- Google deprecated FAQ rich results in May 2026;
- Google removed the FAQ rich-result documentation in June 2026;
- therefore FAQ schema should **not** be treated as a search-result visibility feature for this project.

Visible FAQ content remains useful for users and long-tail relevance.

Do not use `QAPage` for the project's FAQ: Google explicitly reserves QAPage for pages where users can submit alternative answers.

# 6. Credential verification — Laila Del Monte

This is a high-priority trust/SEO issue.

The client brief states:
- four years of formation;
- “Diplôme de l'école de Laila Del Monté”.

Current official Laila Del Monte site states, among other points:
- no communication-animal professional is “certified by Laila Del Monte”;
- it refers to a training certificate / school process;
- the communication-animal training does not train practitioners in energetic care;
- the communication-animal training does not authorize a claim of training for communication with deceased animals.

Therefore:

## Mandatory public-copy rule

Do not publish wording such as:
- “certifiée par Laila Del Monte”
- “formée par Laila Del Monte pour les soins énergétiques”
- “formée par Laila Del Monte pour communiquer avec les défunts”

without exact documentary evidence and explicit approval.

Before launch, obtain the exact credential/certificate/diploma wording from Laure and reproduce it accurately.

Relevant official sources reviewed:
- https://lailadelmonte.com/
- https://lailadelmonte.com/formation-a-la-communication-animale-en-ligne/

# 7. Current Google Search rules relevant to the project

## Titles

Google recommends:
- unique;
- descriptive;
- concise;
- no keyword stuffing;
- clear main-page title.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link

## Meta descriptions

Google primarily builds snippets from page content and may use the meta description when it is a better summary.

Use:
- one unique page-specific summary;
- no keyword lists;
- no fake guarantees.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet

## Helpful / AI-assisted content

Google's October 2026 guidance emphasizes:
- original effort;
- accuracy;
- clear authorship;
- people-first purpose;
- avoiding mass-produced automated pages.

Journal content must be reviewed/owned by Laure or an identified human editor before publication.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Article markup

`Article` / `BlogPosting` structured data remains supported and can help Google understand:
- headline;
- image;
- dates;
- author.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article

## FAQ rich result

The feature no longer appears in Google Search from May 2026.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/updates

## noindex

Noindex pages must remain crawlable so Google can see the noindex directive.

Do not block confirmation/thank-you pages in robots.txt if they rely on meta noindex.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing

## Canonicals

Use one preferred canonical URL for each indexable page and avoid duplicate Timetics/service URLs.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization

## Images / multimodal search

Google added multimodal Search performance reporting to Search Console in September 2026.

Because this brand is image-led, original artwork should be treated as an SEO asset:
- contextual surrounding text;
- useful filenames;
- appropriate alt text;
- stable image URLs;
- responsive optimized derivatives;
- dimensions/aspect ratio reserved.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/09/web-multimodal-in-sc
